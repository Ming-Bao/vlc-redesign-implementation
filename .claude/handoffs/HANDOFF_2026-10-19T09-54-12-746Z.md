## Decisions
- **The panel shares the global `History`.** The alternative was a second NavigationHistory, which would mean touching the 27 files that call `History`.
  - Shared works because `MainInterface`'s `viewLoader` swaps Player and MainDisplay, so they are never alive together.
  - ↗ just sets `MainCtx.playerView = false`; the library then reloads `History.viewPath`.
- **Shared loader:** `maininterface/qml/LibraryPageLoader.qml` holds pageModel, sort memory and history wiring, plus `openSection(uri)` and `viewLoaded()`. `LibraryAddMenu.qml` is the Add item menu. Both MainDisplay and the player panel (`player/qml/LibraryPanel.qml`) use them.
- **Rail:** `SideNavigationPane` with `delegateShowText: false`.
- **Panel width:** `min(dp(427), window/2)`.
- **Panel margins:** the page area's right margin stays at the pages' own 32, not Figma's 20, so the scroll bar stays inside. Rail icons sit 1 px right of Figma (shared padding).
- **Escape** at the history root focuses the title-bar toggle (`navigationCancel()`); the panel stays open.
- **Active sidebar entry (user said "use the figma"):** a filled glyph, in both the rail and the library sidebar.
  - Accent at a section's root, grey deeper; the root label is `#C6C6C6`.
  - Helper `Helpers.sidebarEntryState(viewPath, uri)` returns 0/1/2.
  - New glyph keys: `home_filled`, `videos_filled`, `music_filled`, `network_filled`, `playlists_filled`, `recent_filled` (same glyph as the outline recent, since Figma has none), `open_in_library`, and `music_folder` (the sidebar's Music outline; `topbar_music` stays the note used elsewhere).
- **Compact header:** `PageExt` / `DefaultPageHeader` switch to the Figma two-row header by width (< dp(600)), not by a flag. Search folds into an icon. ↗ shows only when `MainCtx.playerView`; its action copies Player.qml's `leavePlayer()`.
- **Playing mark (user said yes):** reuses 4c's existing `HomeListRow.playing` look (accent title, play mark, 70% overlay). Now also on HomeSquareCard and HomeVideoCard, Home's rows and cards, and grid cards. Matching goes through `Helpers.sameMedia(mrl, Player.url.toString())`.
- **Skipped** the roadmap's "before 4e" grid-page extraction: the panel didn't need to change those pages.
- **Title bar fix:** `menuBtn` and `navigationVisibilty` in `LocalTopbar.qml` now get `anchors.verticalCenter`.
